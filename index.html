Frequently Asked Questions

How do I preview full-resolution images and EXIF metrics? +
Click or tap any photograph in the gallery grid, 3D cylindrical horizon, wave matrix, or infinite tunnel to open the Lightbox Theater. Full verified optical EXIF parameters are displayed alongside the image.
Are my saved favorites synchronized across devices? +
Favorites are stored locally inside your browser's private Web Storage (`localStorage`). They persist through page reloads without requiring an account.
How do the 3D Panoramic Arc and Infinite Tunnel work? +
Both components are built with 100% Vanilla JavaScript and CSS 3D Transforms (`perspective`, `rotateY`, `rotateZ`, `translateZ`). The infinite tunnel uses smooth exponential Lerp damping on scroll, and the panoramic arc applies spherical cylindrical projection.
Can I use the images for commercial projects? +
All images in the VISTARA gallery are provided under an open license. They are free to use for both personal and commercial projects, provided they are not directly resold or claimed as your own photography.
What technology powers the Multilayer 3D Floating Engine? +
The floating kinetic depth engine uses pure Vanilla JavaScript physics calculations. It hooks into a high-performance requestAnimationFrame loop, dynamically calculating mouse vectors and applying CSS 3D transforms (`translate3d`, `rotateX`, `rotateY`) for buttery smooth multi-axis parallax.
Does the gallery support Light/Dark themes? +
Yes. VISTARA features a bespoke dual-theme architecture. Our premium "Obsidian & Gold" dark theme is designed for optimal cinematic image viewing, while the "Ivory & Obsidian" light theme provides a crisp, high-fashion editorial aesthetic.
